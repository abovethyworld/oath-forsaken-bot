# OSRS Lore & Dark Storytelling Reference

Created 2026-10-13. Verified lore and storytelling patterns for Oath Forsaken content.

> **HARD RULE (Gavin 2026-10-13): Only quote OSRS lore. Never RS3, never Dragonwilds.** If a lore detail only exists in RS3 (e.g. Battle of Lumbridge, Moia's Rift backstory), it does not go in. When in doubt, verify against oldschool.runescape.wiki only.

## Zamorak Lore (verified)

- Zamorak was a Mahjarrat, highest-ranking general under Zaros (Legatus Maximus)
- Rebelled due to stagnation in the Zarosian Empire
- Drove the Staff of Armadyl through Zaros's chest, absorbed his power
- Used the Stone of Jas in conjunction with the Staff to attain godhood
- Did NOT inherit or earn godhood — seized it by force
- God of chaos, power, destruction, personal ambition, change
- Colors: red, black, gold
- Key theme: strength is the only truth, chaos is freedom, take what you want

## Zaros Lore (verified)

- Built an empire on order, called it eternal
- Drained Mahjarrat power, saw them as tools
- Betrayed by his foremost general (Zamorak)

## Storytelling Voice for Oath Forsaken

- Short, direct sentences. No fluff.
- Second person ("You did..."). Makes it personal.
- Dark but not edgy-for-edgy's-sake. Earned darkness.
- Zamorakian themes: choosing the hard path, refusing to kneel, taking power
- End with the rank name alone as a punchline ("Ascendant.")
- Iron stories: about the grind (no GE, dry streaks, hours)
- Main stories: about choosing to stay when you don't have to

## The Ages (verified)

### First Age (~4,000 years) — The Age of Creation
- Guthix discovers Gielinor as a "blank plane" and shapes it
- Brings worthy races through the World Gate: humans, dwarves, gnomes
- Seren brings elves from Tarddiad, builds Prifddinas (oldest city still standing)
- Guthix uses the Stone of Jas to create runestones, scattering magic across the land
- Mid-age: Guthix descends underground, begins ~8,000 year slumber
- Late age: other gods begin arriving

### Second Age (~2,000 years) — The Zarosian Empire
- Begins when Guthix sleeps; gods arrive seeking domination
- Zaros arrives near Senntisten (year 3427), builds the greatest empire Gielinor has seen
- His forces: Mahjarrat, Dragon Riders, demons, vampyres, humans
- Saradomin (humans, Icyene), Armadyl (humans, aviansie), Bandos, Tumeken, Elidinis also claim territory
- At its peak, Zamorak — Zaros's highest general — drives the Staff of Armadyl through his master's chest
- Zamorak is banished by the other gods, returns, declares war

### Third Age (~4,000 years) — The God Wars
- Begins with Zamorak's return as a god, warring to reclaim the Stone of Jas (stolen by Saradomin)
- Millions dead; dozens of cities destroyed; references to Zaros nearly erased
- Races driven to near-extinction: aviansie, icyene, ourg, centaurs, wyrms
- Saradomin, Armadyl, and Bandos forge the Godsword to kill Zamorak
- K'ril Tsutsaroth leads the Zamorakian assault on the Godsword transport
- Cornered in Forinthry, Zamorak unleashes the Stone of Jas — incinerates the entire region
- The Anima Mundi cries out; Guthix wakes, slams his sword into Forinthry, establishes the Edicts
- All major gods banished from direct interference

### Fourth Age — The Age of Mortals
- Gods gone; mortals rebuild without divine interference
- Kingdoms rise: Misthalin, Asgarnia, Kandarin take shape

### Fifth Age — The Age of Heroes (the OSRS "now")
- Adventurers rise; the old powers stir beneath the surface
- Ends when Sliske murders Guthix with the Staff of Armadyl, shattering the Edicts

### Sixth Age — The Divine Age
- Edicts broken; gods return (Saradomin first)
- Largely RS3 territory; mentioned for completeness

## Major Gods (verified)

### Elder Gods (brief)
- Mah: youngest Elder God, thought stillborn by the others. Created Zaros and Seren on Freneskae from her own dwindling energy. Primitive instincts; treated her creations as toys. Sleeps in Freneskae's volcano; her nightmares shake the world.
- Jas: namesake of the Stone of Jas. The other Elder Gods are distant history — what matters for Zamorakian lore is that the young gods are all playing with tools they didn't make.

### Zaros — The Empty Lord (expanded)
- Created by Mah on Freneskae alongside Seren (his light opposite)
- God of fate and control; built an empire on absolute order
- Saw the Mahjarrat as tools, drained their power, called them mercenaries to their faces
- His capital Senntisten was the center of the known world
- Fled Gielinor bodiless when Zamorak struck; empire crumbled without him
- Key theme: order taken to its limit becomes tyranny — the stagnation Zamorak rebelled against

### Saradomin — The Rival
- God of order and wisdom. Was once human; ascended long ago
- Followers: humans (most churches on Gielinor are his), Icyene (winged humanoids, nearly wiped out in the God Wars)
- Symbol: four-tipped star. Colors: blue, white, gold
- Stole the Stone of Jas from Zamorak — the spark of the God Wars
- Zamorak's arch-enemy and philosophical opposite: Saradomin believes order and wisdom elevate mortals; Zamorak believes order causes stagnation and only chaos/struggle unlocks potential
- Defeated Zamorak at the Battle of Lumbridge (Sixth Age); first god to return after Guthix died
- Zamorakian view: his "goodness" is propaganda — he destroyed the Naragi realm and the city of Askroth

### Armadyl — God of Justice
- Avian god; followers are the aviansie (bird people) and humans
- Holds a grudge against Zamorak for the near-extinction of the aviansie in the God Wars
- Allied with Saradomin and Bandos to forge the Godsword
- Zamorakian note: the most "amicable" of the enemy gods — Zamorak admitted he would have negotiated with Armadyl if Saradomin hadn't been there

### Bandos — God of War
- Believes in conflict for its own sake (contrast: Zamorak believes destruction must serve a purpose)
- Changed sides repeatedly in the God Wars; joined the anti-Zamorak triumvirate
- Clamored for Zamorak's death at the end

### Seren — Goddess of Light
- Created by Mah alongside Zaros; his opposite in every way
- Brought the elves to Tirannwn, built Prifddinas
- Opposed Zaros's plan to ascend to Elder Godhood (feared it would wake the Elder Gods)
- Chose to stay with Mah on Freneskae when Zaros fled

### Guthix — The Balancer
- Found Gielinor, shaped it, gave it magic via the Stone of Jas
- God of balance and nature; did NOT want worship — "no better than any of the other young gods"
- Slept through the Second Age; woke to the God Wars' devastation
- Established the Edicts: no god may directly interfere on Gielinor again
- Hid the Stone of Jas deep underground
- Murdered by Sliske with the Staff of Armadyl — his death ended the Fifth Age and broke the Edicts

## Key Races & Factions (verified)

### Mahjarrat
- Creations of Mah on Freneskae; a tribe of powerful magicians raised in endless tribal warfare
- Came to Gielinor first as Menaphite mercenaries, then joined Zaros, then split between gods
- The Ritual of Rejuvenation: every ~500 years they sacrifice one of their own to restore the rest — siphoning Mah's energy through a gateway to Freneskae
- Key figures:
  - **Azzanadra**: Zarosian loyalist, high priest. Imprisoned in the Ancient Pyramid after Zaros fell. Returned in the Fifth Age.
  - **Hazeel**: Zamorakian warlord. Has an active cult. Schemer.
  - **Moia**: Zamorak's general. Rescued Zamorak after his defeat at the Battle of Lumbridge. Fiercely loyal.
  - **Lucien**: sought godhood via the Stone of Jas. Touched it, declared "I AM A GOD" — killed by Dragonkin for misusing it.
  - **Sliske**: Zarosian trickster. Freed Nex, murdered Guthix, ran the gods' tournament. Treats war as a game.
  - **Zemouregal**: Zamorakian, schemer, tried to raise an undead army under Varrock

### Kinshra (Black Knights)
- Human followers of Zamorak; sworn enemies of Saradomin's White Knights
- Based at the Black Knights' Fortress; led by Lord Daquarius
- Fight "in the name of chaos" — the human face of Zamorakianism
- Zamorak personally summoned Daquarius to help steal the Stone of Jas from Sliske — proof the god still calls on his mortal followers
- Zamorakian writing angle: ordinary humans who chose chaos over comfort

### Demons
- Homeworld is Infernus (also called Pandemonium) — jagged rock and liquid fire
- **Avernic**: the demons who sided with Zamorak. Thammaron and Zebub (Tsutsaroth demons) joined Zamorak's rebellion against Zaros, carried his broken body to Infernus after the betrayal, then spent 19 years helping him overthrow the Chthonian rulers. Reward: Zamorak's eternal favor. K'ril Tsutsaroth later commanded Zamorak's armies in the God Wars.
- **Chthonic**: ruled Infernus under Hostilius the Autocrat until Zamorak and the Avernic overthrew them
- Key theme: Zamorak FREED the Avernic from Chthonian rule — their loyalty is earned, not compelled

### Vampyres
- From Vampyrium; came to Gielinor serving Zaros, then flipped to Zamorak
- **Lord Lowerniel Drakan**: joined Zamorak's rebellion against Zaros. Reward: the kingdom of Hallowvale — which he twisted into Morytania (the swamp)
- Conquered Hallowvale from the Saradominist Icyene queen Efaritay
- Imposed blood tithes on human settlements; built the Great Vyrewall
- Werewolves serve under vampyre rule (founded Canifis under Lord Malak)
- Zamorakian angle: they chose Zamorak over Zaros — and were rewarded with a kingdom

### Werewolves
- Serve the vampyres in Morytania; founded the village of Canifis
- Zamorakian by association — part of Drakan's host

## Key Locations (verified)

### Freneskae
- Mahjarrat homeworld. Volcanic wasteland of ash and fire.
- "Realm of constant warfare" — the Mahjarrat tribes fought endlessly; the strong ruled, the weak were sacrificed
- Mah sleeps in the great volcano; her nightmares cause earthquakes
- Why it matters: this is where Zamorak was forged. Everything he believes — strength, struggle, no mercy — comes from Freneskae.

### Pandemonium (Infernus)
- Demon homeworld. Described as jagged rocks and liquid fire, "more like Freneskae than Gielinor"
- Ruled by Infernals, then Chthonians under Hostilius, then liberated by Zamorak and the Avernic
- Zaros once tricked Hostilius into a binding contract for twelve demon legions — then revealed he predated the demon race itself, humiliating him

### Senntisten
- Zaros's capital; the center of his empire and the known world in the Second Age
- "Had become its own purpose, not just an administrative centre but a great living creature"
- Fell to combined Saradominist and Zamorakian forces in the God Wars
- Now buried; excavated by the Archaeology Guild
- Writing angle: the corpse of perfect order

### The Wilderness (Forinthry)
- Was Forinthry: a lush northern kingdom, heartland of the Zarosian Empire
- Zamorak, cornered by Saradomin/Armadyl/Bandos, unleashed the Stone of Jas in a last strike — incinerated the entire region
- Nothing grows there. The land is scarred and cursed.
- Guthix slammed his sword into its center (the Wilderness Volcano) as a warning when he established the Edicts
- Why it's wild: god-tier destruction doesn't heal. The Wilderness is a wound that never closed — perfect Zamorakian territory.

## Key Artifacts (verified)

### Staff of Armadyl
- Elder Artefact. Impossibly powerful.
- Zamorak used it to stab Zaros and steal his divinity
- Shattered by the Dragonkin Sakirth when killing Lucien (who had misused the Stone of Jas)
- Repaired by Sliske; later used by Sliske to murder Guthix
- Writing angle: the weapon that has killed a god, made a god, and killed the god of balance — it keeps changing history

### Stone of Jas
- Elder Artefact. Guthix used it to create runestone magic.
- Zamorak seized it; Saradomin stole it from him (spark of the God Wars)
- Zamorak reclaimed it and used it to destroy Forinthry
- Guthix hid it deep underground after the God Wars; guarded by the Crux Eqal
- Lucien touched it and declared godhood — the Dragonkin killed him for the misuse
- Sliske stole it and offered it as a tournament prize between the gods
- Destroyed in the Sixth Age
- Writing angle: the most dangerous object on Gielinor — every god who touches it breaks something

## Oathplate Armour (verified 2026-10-13)

- Zamorakian melee armor set: helm, chest, legs. 78 Defence to wear.
- Drops from Yama, a Zamorakian demon. Also craftable (78 Mining, 83 Smithing) from infernal shale + oathplate shards.
- Counts as Zamorakian items for God Wars Dungeon (because Yama is Zamorakian).
- The name says it: oath + plate. Armor you swear yourself to.
- Clan connection: Gavin uses oathplate as the clan's visual imagery. The armor IS the oath made visible.
- Writing angle: you don't buy oathplate, you earn it from a demon — same way you don't join Oath Forsaken, you prove you belong.

## Clan Founding Myth (FINAL 2026-10-13 - reference doc)

"Everyone here served another god once. Saradomin's light. Armadyl's justice. Whatever they promised. We broke those oaths.

Not out of hate. Out of truth. We looked at the easy path they offered and chose the harder one. Zamorak doesn't ask for loyalty — he recognizes those who've already chosen.

We are the Oath Forsaken. We broke our vows to follow something real."

Notes:
- Works for mains and irons (everyone made the same choice)
- "Oath Forsaken" is literal: they forsook oaths to other gods
- Ties to oathplate imagery (the oath made visible)

"You walked into Zamorak's fortress wearing the oathplate. You'd spent months preparing — tracked down Emberlight, the demonbane blade, because you knew what was waiting.
